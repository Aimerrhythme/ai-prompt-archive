```text
Create a 30-second ultra-realistic cinematic fantasy story set on a rainy European city street.

SCENE 1 (0–5 seconds):
A little girl wearing a dark maroon coat stands alone on a rainy city sidewalk. A group of fashionable young adults walks past her without noticing her. A fresh golden-brown bread roll lies on a piece of paper near the wet pavement. Show a close-up of the bread.

SCENE 2 (5–10 seconds):
The little girl looks sad and hungry. She sits near a stone building, looking down. Cut to a beautiful young woman with long black hair, wearing a black blazer and white shirt, standing outside an elegant shop and eating potato chips from a yellow packet.

SCENE 5 (20–25 seconds):
Cut back to the little girl wearing the maroon coat. The magical bread gently arrives in her hands. She looks surprised and emotional, wipes her face, and carefully holds the warm bread.

SCENE 6 (25–30 seconds):
The little girl notices a small hungry gray kitten sitting beside the stone wall. She breaks off a piece of bread and gently offers it to the kitten. The kitten starts eating. The girl smiles warmly. End with a beautiful emotional cinematic shot of the girl and kitten together.

Photorealistic cinematic storytelling, emotional fantasy atmosphere, rainy European streets, warm bakery lighting, magical floating food effects, realistic human expressions, detailed wet pavement reflections, smooth camera tracking, 4K quality, vertical 16:9

No face morphing, no character changes, no unnatural movements.
```
