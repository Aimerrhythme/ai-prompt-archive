## prompt 原文

```text
Create an ultra-photorealistic cinematic historical romantic-comedy video set inside a traditional Joseon-era Korean house during a warm afternoon. Recreate the elegant atmosphere of a hanok room with sliding paper doors, carved wooden screens, lacquered cabinets, painted folding screens with peonies, soft lantern light and silk cushions.
Scene 1 (0–5 seconds): The Challenge
Begin with a wide, symmetrical shot of the sunlit room. A young woman in her mid-20s wearing a deep-red jeogori with a navy hanbok skirt, her black hair neatly tied with a teal ribbon, faces a young man in a flowing white hanbok with his hair in a topknot. Each holds a wooden practice sword. She taps the floor with hers and says: "Pick up your sword, scholar." He sighs: "I came here to read poetry, not to fight."
Scene 2 (5–10 seconds): The First Clash
She lunges and he blocks clumsily, staggering back with exaggerated surprise, his sleeves flapping. Fast, playful cuts of wooden swords clacking, her focused, pouting expression and his comically wide eyes. The paper doors glow behind them in soft blue light.
Scene 3 (10–15 seconds): Her Stubborn Face
Close-ups: she scrunches her nose and puffs her cheeks, determined, then mischievously smirks as she plans her next move. He lifts a folding fan, smiling confidently. She says: "Then you'll lose at both." He grins and fans himself calmly, which only makes her more determined.
Scene 4 (15–20 seconds): The Trick
She pretends to bow politely, and as he relaxes she sweeps his ankle with the wooden sword. He loses his balance and tumbles backward onto the floor cushions with a theatrical gasp. She jumps up and hits a triumphant pose with her sword raised, her skirt swaying.
Scene 5 (20–25 seconds): The Surprise
He lies on his back laughing and says: "Fine. You win." She leans over him, victorious, holding her sword near his chin. Their eyes meet and the playful mood softens. Slow close-up of their faces, her smile fading into a shy blush, a soft glow from the lantern across her cheek.
Scene 6 (25–30 seconds): Peace
He gently lowers her sword with his hand and says: "You fight better than any scholar I know." She laughs and helps him sit up. They sit side by side on the cushions, sharing the fan and laughing quietly as petals drift in through the open door. Slow pull-back to a wide shot of the warm hanok room, then fade out.
Visual Style & Cinematography
Ultra-photorealistic Joseon-era romantic comedy with a warm, playful and elegant tone.
Soft diffused light through paper doors with cool blue shadows and warm lantern glow.
Symmetrical wide shots, gentle tracking, expressive close-ups and light slow motion on the funniest moments.
Period-accurate hanbok, hairstyles, wooden swords, folding fans, furniture and decor, with natural silk fabric movement.
Consistent faces, hairstyles and outfits for both characters throughout.
Audio: wooden sword clacks, rustling silk, a soft traditional gayageum-and-flute melody with a light comic rhythm, clear natural English dialogue.
Wholesome, non-graphic, family-friendly. No text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark.
```
